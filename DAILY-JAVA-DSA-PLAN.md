# 🎯 Study Rules

## During Pomodoro (25 min):
1. **Phone face down** - No notifications
2. **Code yourself** - Type every line, don't copy-paste
3. **Comment as you go** - Explain what each section does
4. **Run and test** - After writing, always test the code
5. **If stuck for >10 min** - Look up syntax, but type the solution yourself

## During Breaks (5 min):
1. **Stand up** - Don't stay seated
2. **Look away from screen** - Rest your eyes
3. **Drink water** - Stay hydrated
4. **Don't check social media** - Will break your focus

## DSA Hour (Non-negotiable):
- This builds your problem-solving muscle
- Even if tired, do at least 2 problems
- Quality > Quantity (1 problem understood deeply > 5 rushed)
